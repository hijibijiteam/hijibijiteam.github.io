Who We Are / page 1

We're a small team — three people, sometimes four, depends on exam season. We write things down before we build them. The good ideas usually show up in the margins, next to the doodles, not in the "official" plan.

We build tools that actually work on Monday morning. We've burned our hands on enough 2am bugs to know better now. Operating out of Bogra, Bangladesh.
